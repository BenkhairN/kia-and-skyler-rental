Admin Website Guide

Managing vehicles, bookings, customers, feedback and settings

Grand Bahama Island · November 2026

1. Getting Started

Logging in

  1. Go to your website and scroll to the bottom of any page, then click Admin — or go directly to yourwebsite.com/admin.
  2. Enter your admin username and password.
  3. You will land on the Dashboard.
Keep your admin login private. Anyone with these credentials can see bookings, edit vehicles, and change prices. To change your password, ask your developer.

Finding your way around

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick snapshot:

  • Total vehicles — how many cars are in your fleet and how many are available right now
  • Active & confirmed — confirmed bookings, plus pending requests waiting on you
  • Revenue — total from active and completed rentals
  • Pending requests — new booking requests waiting for confirmation
  • Revenue by month — bar chart of the last 6 months
  • Recent bookings — the 6 newest requests; click one to open it in Bookings

3. Vehicles

Adding a vehicle

  1. Go to Vehicles → Add vehicle.
  2. Fill in the details: name, year, color, category, seats, transmission, fuel type, daily rate and features.
  3. Upload photos — the first photo becomes the cover photo shown on the website.
  4. Click Save. The vehicle appears on the public site immediately.

Editing a vehicle

Click the vehicle's Edit button in the Vehicles list. Change any details and save. Changes go live instantly.

Photos

  • Upload multiple photos per vehicle — customers see them as a gallery on the car's page.
  • Hover a photo to set it as cover, reorder or delete it.
  • Use clear, horizontal photos — around 1600×1000 pixels looks best.

Maintenance mode

If a vehicle is being repaired or unavailable, flip its status toggle to Maintenance. The car stays listed but shows as unavailable so customers can't book it.

Availability is automatic. The website checks booking dates — a car with a confirmed booking for those dates can't be double-booked. You don't need to mark cars as "rented" manually.

4. Bookings

This is where you spend most of your time. The table shows every rental request:

ColumnWhat's in it
CustomerName, phone number, and booking reference stacked together
VehicleThe requested car and its color
DatesPick-up date, return date, and when the booking was made
TotalAmount due, and how they'll pay: At pick-up (gold) or Paid online (green)
StatusDropdown to change the booking status
ActionsOpen details, edit, or delete

The booking workflow

Pending→ Confirmed→ Completed
  1. Customer submits a request — it arrives as Pending. You get a notification popup if you're logged in.
  2. Contact the customer — call or WhatsApp them to confirm dates, price and pick-up location.
  3. Change status to Confirmed — only after you've spoken with them. The customer can now see "Confirmed" when they check their booking.
  4. "Picked up" appears automatically — during the rental period, the booking shows as picked up on the customer's status page. No action needed.
  5. After return, set to Completed — marks the rental finished.
  6. Cancelled — if the customer cancels or the request doesn't work out, set it to Cancelled. The car becomes bookable for those dates again.

Editing a booking

Open a booking and click Edit to change the vehicle, dates, customer details, pick-up location, notes, status and payment info.

Payment method

  • Customers choose Pay online or I'll pay at pick-up on their booking status page after you confirm.
  • The Total column shows their choice: At pick-up or Paid online.
  • If someone chose pick-up but wants to pay online later, they can switch on their status page — you'll see it update.

Search & sort

  • Use the search box to find a booking by name, phone, reference or vehicle.
  • Click column headers to sort by date, total, etc.

5. Calendar

A month view of your rentals:

  • Each colored chip is a booking — the color shows its status (pending, confirmed, picked up, completed).
  • The chip spans every day from pick-up through the return date.
  • Click a chip to jump to that booking in the Bookings list.
  • Use the arrows to move between months; This month jumps back to today.
Use the calendar before confirming a booking — it's the fastest way to spot a vehicle that's already out.

6. Customers

A card per customer showing their name, contact details, how many bookings they've made, and total spent. Useful for recognizing repeat customers.

7. Feedback

Visitors can leave messages and star ratings through the floating feedback box on the website.

  • Unread messages are highlighted with a gold border and dot.
  • Click a message to read the full text — opening it marks it as read.
  • Each message shows the sender's name, rating and any contact info they left.

9. How a customer books (for reference)

  1. Browses Cars, opens a vehicle, picks dates on the availability calendar.
  2. Clicks Book, enters their name, phone, email, pick-up location and dates.
  3. Gets a 10-character reference (like A4F9C2E1B7) — they use it to check their status.
  4. On Check booking they enter the reference + phone number to see their status.
  5. After you confirm, they choose Pay online or Pay at pick-up.

10. Quick tips

  • Always call/WhatsApp the customer before confirming — confirming tells them the booking is real.
  • The booking reference + phone is the "password" to look up a booking — tell customers to screenshot it.
  • If a car is in the shop, use Maintenance — don't delete it.
  • Deleting a booking removes it permanently. Prefer Cancelled for real bookings so you keep a record.
  • Everything updates live — a customer looking at their status sees changes the moment you make them.
